How I make it

One brief, one emotion, one story.

I do not force a product into a story. I find the human emotion inside your brief, build the story around it, and let the product be the solution.

  1. Emotion. What should the viewer feel before they want the product? One video carries one dominant emotion.
  2. Story. A relatable situation that naturally creates that feeling, with a hook about the person, not the product.
  3. Product and proof. The product arrives as the solution. Every claim in your brief is shown, not just said.
  4. Recommendation. The story closes on the change and a natural recommendation, then your call to action.
